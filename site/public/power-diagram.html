Open decisions before schemdraw

  • Charger tie-in — into BMS P+/P- (pack side, as drawn) vs downstream of the fuse so charge current is fused too.
  • Fuse placement — right at P+ (protects everything) vs load-branch only.
  • "Docked" sense — ESP32 reads the dock contacts vs the charger output (feeds the map-pose auto-dock plan).
  • Common ground — all grounds (motors, logic, sense) tie to pack -; draw it explicitly.
